Ask about your project

Project enquiries

Starting a conversation

What information should I send for a quotation?

Please include the project or building location, the service you need, a short description of the work, your target date and the best way to contact you. If available, attach relevant drawings, equipment schedules, photographs, specifications or an existing system list. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Do you need to visit the site before pricing?

It depends on the scope and the information provided. Existing conditions, access, routes, equipment compatibility or repair work may need a site survey to define quantities and assumptions. The requirement can be discussed when the enquiry is reviewed.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

The requirement and available project details need to be reviewed so any missing information, site survey or scope questions can be identified. Before proceeding, review the written quotation for included works, exclusions, assumptions, programme and commercial terms.

How soon will I receive a response or quotation?

Response and pricing times can vary with scope complexity, site-survey needs, project information and availability. No fixed turnaround is promised on this page; share your deadline in the enquiry so it can be considered.

Does the website enquiry form send an email?

The form prepares an email draft in your device's email app; it does not send the enquiry automatically. Review the draft and press Send, or use the phone, email or WhatsApp details on the Contact page.

Services & scope

Understanding the work

The proposed scope and availability should be confirmed for the specific project.

Can several services be included in one proposal?

Related services can be discussed together. Whether they can be included in one proposal depends on the project requirements, interfaces, schedule and confirmed scope. Ask for inclusions and exclusions to be shown clearly in writing.

Can you work with an existing building or system?

Existing-system work may be possible depending on equipment condition, access to documentation, manufacturer or protocol compatibility, parts availability and the requested changes. Provide model details and clear photographs where possible; a site assessment may be required.

What is usually included in an ELV AMC?

There is no universal inclusion. The agreement should identify covered systems and equipment, planned visits, inspection activities, call-out or response arrangements, spare parts, exclusions and charges for additional work. Confirm all service terms in the written proposal or contract.

Can a BMS integrate every building system?

Integration is not automatic. It depends on each system's available interface, supported protocol, licences, design information, permissions and agreed points list. Confirm compatibility and interface responsibilities before committing to an integrated scope.

Is a public address system the same as an emergency voice alarm system?

Not necessarily. General public address and emergency voice-alarm systems can have different design, equipment, approval and testing requirements. State the required function clearly so the applicable project requirements can be reviewed.

Delivery & operation

Planning the next steps

What affects the programme and price?

Scope and quantities, site conditions, materials and equipment, access, working hours, coordination, approvals, testing requirements and lead times can all affect a project. A quotation should state its assumptions and exclusions so the basis of the proposal is clear.

Who handles permits, approvals and authority requirements?

Responsibility depends on the project, contract and applicable requirements. Confirm in writing which party is responsible for design submissions, permits, approvals, inspections, utility coordination and related fees before work starts.

What handover documents should I expect?

Handover depends on the agreed work and project requirements. It may include relevant test results, equipment or configuration information, operating guidance and maintenance recommendations. Confirm the required format and deliverables in the scope.

Do you provide maintenance after installation?

Maintenance can be discussed as a separate service or contract. Covered systems, planned visits, response arrangements, parts and exclusions need to be agreed for the particular building and installation.
