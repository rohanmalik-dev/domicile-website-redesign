What should I include in a quotation enquiry?

Share the building location, service needed, a short scope description and any useful drawings, schedules or site photographs. Mention your target date and preferred contact method.

Can several building services be discussed together?

Yes. Related scopes can be discussed together; the proposed package depends on site needs, interfaces, programme and confirmed responsibilities.

Will the enquiry form send my message automatically?

No. This static website prepares an email draft in your email app. Review the details and press Send, or contact the team directly by phone or WhatsApp.

Are project timelines and maintenance plans fixed?

They depend on the confirmed scope, site conditions and project requirements. The written proposal should set out relevant deliverables, assumptions and support arrangements.
